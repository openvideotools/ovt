more regular external contributors, the owner intends to move to a technical steering group of three to five
people. Decision rule: lazy consensus on pull requests, a majority of maintainers on ADR changes, and the
owner's veto on licensing and trademark. [`docs/dev/governance.md`](docs/dev/governance.md) has the details.
