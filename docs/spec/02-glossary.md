# 2. Glossary

This chapter defines every term used in the specification that an implementer might not already know, merged from the "Glossary additions" of chapters 3–15 and from the backbone seed list (§0.9). Entries are one alphabetical list (A–Z, then entries starting with digits or symbols). Each entry has the form `**Term** (aliases) — definition. [Spec: … | OVT: …]`: the bracket names the external specification clause that defines the term (`H.264 §x`, `H.265 §x`, `AV1 §x`, `14496-12 §x`, and so on, following the citation rules of chapter 1 §1.8) and/or the OVT chapter and section where the term is used normatively. Where an OVT schema field, enum or C/C++ identifier carries the concept, the entry names it in backticks (for example `CellGrid.qp`); such identifiers are copied from the chapter that owns them (chapter 6 for the data model, 7 for bitstream and plugins, 8 for containers, 9 for metrics, 10 for rendering, 11 for the GUI, 12 for the CLI and C API). When chapters use a term with genuinely different meanings, the entry gives the normative meaning first and notes the other usage as "(chapter N uses the term as …)".

Codec-neutral vocabulary: OVT uses **block** for any codec's coding unit (H.264 *macroblock*, HEVC *CTU/CU/PU/TU*, AV1 *superblock/block/transform block*) and **cell** for the 4×4 luma-sample square that is the smallest addressable analysis unit. Codec-specific terms keep the spelling of their own specification. Unit conventions used in definitions: positions and sizes are in luma samples in *coded-picture coordinates* (origin top-left, before cropping); motion vectors in the neutral model are in 1/16 luma-sample units; bit positions count from the first bit of the owning unit's RBSP (H.26x) or OBU header (AV1); byte positions count from the start of the file; luminance is in cd/m² (nits); entropy cost is in 1/8-bit units (`bits_x8`). Value ranges are given where a quantity has a fixed range (e.g. QP 0–51 for H.264 8-bit, `qindex` 0–255 for AV1).

## A

- **AAB** (Android App Bundle) — Google Play's upload format (`.aab`) from which Play generates per-device APKs; OVT uploads AABs to Play and plain APKs to F-Droid and GitHub releases. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.4.8]
- **abi3 wheel** — a CPython extension wheel built against the Limited API (`Py_LIMITED_API`). OVT does **not** ship abi3 wheels: pybind11 has no stable-ABI build, so the `ovt` package ships *per-interpreter wheels* (`cp311`, `cp312`, `cp313`). [OVT: chapter 12 §12.4.1; chapter 4 FR-API-008]
- **ABI filter** (Android) — the Gradle setting (`ndk.abiFilters`) restricting which CPU ABIs are compiled and packaged; OVT builds `arm64-v8a` and `x86_64` only. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.4.2]
- **ABI version** (plugin ABI version) — the integer a plugin reports in `ovt_AnalysisDecoderApi.abi_version` or `ovt_MetricApi.abi_version`; the host refuses a plugin whose value differs from the compiled-in `OVT_ANALYSIS_ABI_VERSION` (currently 1). A new major value means the struct layout changed. [OVT: chapter 0 §0.6.2–§0.6.3; chapter 7 §7.5.1]
- **Acceptance procedure** — the per-phase, numbered step list that demonstrates the phase exit criterion of chapter 0 §0.8; results are recorded under `docs/acceptance/`. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.11]
- **Acceptance test** — the Given/When/Then line(s) attached to every FR/NFR and work item that a tester or CI can execute; part of the Definition of Done. [OVT: chapter 0 §0.1; chapter 14 §14.1.2]
- **Access unit** (AU) — all NAL units that together decode to exactly one coded picture (H.264/HEVC), including the leading AUD, SEI and parameter-set NAL units; the AV1 analogue is the temporal unit. In OVT a `FrameInfo` is one coded picture and `FrameInfo.units` lists its `Unit` indices. [Spec: H.264 §7.4.1.2.3; H.265 §7.4.2.4.4 | OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.9]
- **Access unit size** (`FrameInfo.size_bytes`) — the byte size OVT attributes to a frame: the bytes of all its units including their start codes or length prefixes and the immediately preceding AUD/SEI/parameter-set units of the access unit (D7; chapter 7 §7.4.2 step S7; chapter 6 §6.4 field comment). [OVT: chapter 7 §7.4.2; chapter 6 §6.3]
- **ActiveSets** — OVT's per-stream table of the most recently received parameter sets by id (SPS/PPS/VPS, AV1 sequence header) together with the activation rules that decide which set a slice or frame uses. [Spec: H.264 §7.4.1.2.1; H.265 §7.4.2.4.2; AV1 §7.5 | OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.8]
- **Adaptive loop filter** (ALF) — the H.266/VVC Wiener-type in-loop filter; not present in H.264/HEVC/AV1 and out of scope for OVT except as a name in the `Codec.H266` placeholder. [Spec: H.266 §8.8.5]
- **ad-hoc signing** — macOS `codesign --sign -` with no developer identity; binaries run locally but cannot be notarised. Used for PR builds. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.5.4]
- **Affine motion** — motion compensation where the MV varies linearly across the block (rotation/zoom); AV1's *warped motion* is its 6-parameter local form. `MotionVector.flags` bit 3 marks sub-block (affine/OBMC) motion. [Spec: AV1 §7.11.3.5 | OVT: chapter 6 §6.3]
- **AHardwareBuffer** — Android's NDK handle to a GPU/compositor-shareable buffer; OVT uses it to pass decoded HDR frames from MediaCodec/Vulkan to the `SurfaceView` without a CPU copy. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.4.3; chapter 13 §13.4]
- **Akima interpolator** — a piecewise-cubic interpolation (Akima 1970) that avoids overshoot; one of the three BD-rate interpolators (`Interpolator.Akima`). [OVT: chapter 9 §9.8.4; chapter 6 §6.5]
- **Alias flag** (CLI) — a spelling of an option kept for muscle memory that maps to the canonical one without separate semantics: `--json`/`--text`/`--flatbuffers` → `--format …`, `decode` → `dump`, `units` → `syntax --list` (chapter 12 §12.2.0 alias table). [OVT: chapter 12 §12.1.2]
- **Alignment** (temporal / spatial) — determining the frame offset (and optionally a pixel shift and crop) between a distorted and a reference stream before frames are paired for a full-reference metric. Output: `offset_frames`, `drift_per_1000`, `confidence`, `status`. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.3]
- **Alignment confidence** — `S(o*) − S(o₂)`: the NCC score of the best offset minus that of the best offset at least one frame away, range 0–2; below `min_confidence` (default 0.05) the job continues but sets `AlignmentReport.status = "low_confidence"` and emits `VQ-ALIGN-0001`. Stored in `MetricsResult.alignment_confidence` (D9; the offset sign is: distorted frame *i* ↔ reference frame *i + offset*, so dropping 7 leading distorted frames gives `+7`). [OVT: chapter 9 §9.3.1]
- **AlignmentMode** — enum in `ovt_metrics.fbs` selecting automatic, fixed-offset or disabled temporal alignment in a `NormalizationConfig`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.5]
- **ALPHA / alpha plane** — a fourth sample plane carrying transparency; AV1 and HEVC auxiliary pictures can carry one. `blob_id` plane token `a`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.6.2]
- **AMVP** (advanced motion vector prediction) — HEVC's explicit inter mode: a 2-candidate MV predictor list is built from spatial/temporal neighbours, an index (`mvp_lX_flag`) and an MVD are signalled. OVT records `MotionVector.predictor = "amvp[i]"`. [Spec: H.265 §8.5.3.2.6–8.5.3.2.8 | OVT: chapter 6 §6.3]
- **Analysis path** — the GOP-local software decode through an analysis plugin that produces `FrameAnalysis` records and plane blobs, as opposed to the *playback path* (ADR-009). [OVT: chapter 5 §5.10; chapter 7 §7.5]
- **Analysis pending** — the GUI state in which block-level data for the current frame has not yet been produced by the analysis path; panels show their "pending" state rather than an error. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.7]
- **Analysis plugin** (analysis decoder plugin) — a shared library exporting `ovt_analysis_decoder_v1()` that decodes units and returns `FrameAnalysis` FlatBuffers plus planes (`ovt-plugin-aom`, `ovt-plugin-de265`, `ovt-plugin-ffmpeg`, `ovt-plugin-hm`). [OVT: chapter 0 §0.6.2; chapter 7 §7.6]
- **android-hdr** — chapter 14 §14.9.2 hardware id of the HDR-capable Android tablet on which Android HDR acceptance tests run (D12); the Pixel Tablet (`pixel-tablet`) has an SDR panel and covers the SDR fallback only. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.9.2; chapter 16 §16.5.3]
- **Annex B** — (1) for H.264/HEVC: the *byte stream format* framing NAL units with `00 00 01` start codes (optional `zero_byte`, `trailing_zero_8bits`); (2) for AV1: the *length-delimited* bitstream format nesting `temporal_unit_size` / `frame_unit_size` / `obu_length` leb128 fields, as opposed to the Section 5 low-overhead format. The two are unrelated; OVT's sniffer distinguishes them. [Spec: H.264 Annex B; H.265 Annex B; AV1 Annex B | OVT: chapter 8 §8.6.3–8.6.4]
- **aomanalyzer** — the Electron-based viewer that consumes libaom `inspect` JSON; OVT exports a compatible subset for AV1. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.8.3]
- **AOM Patent License 1.0** — the royalty-free patent grant covering AV1 implementations, with defensive termination; its text must ship with binaries. [OVT: chapter 15 §15.5]
- **APSNR** (average PSNR) — PSNR computed from the mean MSE over all frames, `10·log10(peak² / mean MSE)`, as in the AOM common test conditions; differs from the mean of per-frame PSNR values. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.4.2]
- **aqtinstall** — a Python tool (`aqt`) that downloads official Qt binary kits by version and module without the Qt installer; used in CI. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.1]
- **ARCVM** — the virtual machine in which ChromeOS runs Android applications; relevant to HDR passthrough and file access on Chromebooks. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.4.7]
- **Area-normalised statistic** — a histogram value expressed as the percentage of luma samples (cells × 16) covered by the counted blocks, as opposed to a count or a bits share. [OVT: chapter 4 FR-BLK-020, FR-GUI-031; chapter 11 §11.3.12]
- **Argon streams** — AOM's exhaustive AV1 conformance suite (by Argon Design) covering every syntax element and decoder branch; large, account-gated download; tag `needs-corpus`. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.3.1]
- **Atom** — QuickTime's name for an ISOBMFF *box*. [Spec: 14496-12 §4.2 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.2.3]
- **AUD** (access unit delimiter) — NAL unit type 9 (H.264) / 35 (HEVC) that optionally starts an access unit; OVT counts its bytes into the following frame's `size_bytes`. [Spec: H.264 §7.3.2.4; H.265 §7.3.2.5 | OVT: chapter 7 §7.4.2]
- **Auxiliary sample information** (`saiz`/`saio`) — per-sample side data located by a size table and offset table; in CENC it holds the per-sample IV and subsample map. [Spec: 14496-12 §8.7.8–8.7.9 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.3 group D]
- **AV1** — the AOMedia Video 1 codec (2018); units are OBUs, blocks are superblocks/blocks/transform blocks, quantisation is `qindex` 0–255. OVT analyses AV1 via `ovt-plugin-aom`. [Spec: AV1 Bitstream & Decoding Process Specification v1.0.0 Errata 1 | OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.6, §7.6.1]
- **Av1DecodeReturn** — the per-frame record (`aom/aomdx.h`: `buf`, `idx`, `show_existing`) that libaom's `decoder_inspect()` writes through `user_priv` on every `aom_codec_decode()` call in inspection mode (one call per frame, D17; the dispatch happens iff `user_priv != NULL`, D30); the analysis plugin pairs it with the `insp_frame_data` delivered by the inspection callback to build `FrameAnalysis` records. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.6.1]
- **AV1 extension tables** — `Av1Block` and `Av1Txb` nested in `Block.ext`, plus `Av1FilterParams`, `Av1CdefParams`, `Av1LrUnit`, `Av1GmParams`, `Av1FilmGrainParams`, `Av1Palette`, `Av1RefState`/`Av1RefSlot` carrying every AV1 syntax element OVT exposes. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3]
- **AVC** (H.264, MPEG-4 Part 10) — ITU-T H.264 / ISO/IEC 14496-10 Advanced Video Coding; blocks are macroblocks, QP 0–51 (+6 per extra bit of depth). [Spec: ITU-T H.264 (08/2021) | OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.4]
- **avcC / hvcC / av1C** — the codec configuration records inside an ISOBMFF sample entry (`AVCDecoderConfigurationRecord`, `HEVCDecoderConfigurationRecord`, `AV1CodecConfigurationRecord`) carrying parameter sets / sequence header and the NAL length-size field; passed to plugins as `codec_private`. [Spec: 14496-15 §5.3.3, §8.3.3; AV1-ISOBMFF §2.3 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.3 group C, §8.7.6]
- **AVC extension tables** — `AvcMb` and `AvcSubMb` nested in `Block.ext`, plus `AvcFilterParams`, `AvcRefMarking`, `AvcMmco`, `AvcRefPic`; enums `AvcMbType`, `AvcSubMbType`, `AvcIntra4x4PredMode`, `AvcIntra16x16PredMode`, `AvcIntraChromaPredMode`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3]
- **AVIF** — AV1 Image File Format: HEIF structure with AV1 items (`avif`/`avis` brands). [Spec: AVIF 1.1 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.3 group F]
- **AVIOContext adapter** — OVT's custom libavformat I/O callbacks that route all demuxer reads through `RandomAccessFile` so Android content URIs and virtual files work. [OVT: chapter 8 §8.7.2]

## B

- **Backbone** (chapter 0) — the architecture backbone every chapter was written against, reprinted as chapter 0 with sections `0.n` (earlier references wrote `B.n`). [OVT: chapter 0]
- **Bar-chart-as-timeline** — OVT's convention of using a per-frame size bar chart as the primary navigation control, realised by the Timeline panel (UX-1, FR-GUI-003). [OVT: chapter 3 §3.5; chapter 11 §11.3.1]
- **Base layer** — the lowest layer of a scalable or Dolby Vision stream, decodable alone; `Unit.layer_id = 0`. [Spec: H.264 Annex G; H.265 Annex F]
- **Baseline (performance)** — stored per-hardware benchmark medians (`tests/perf/baselines/<hardware-id>.json`) against which a regression > 20 % fails CI. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.9.4]
- **BD-rate / BD-PSNR** (Bjøntegaard delta) — the average percentage bitrate difference (BD-rate) or metric difference (BD-PSNR, generally BD-quality) between two RD curves over their overlapping quality (or rate) range, computed by integrating interpolated curves over `log10(bitrate)`. Negative BD-rate = the test saves bitrate. `BdRateResult.bd_rate_percent`, `bd_quality`, `overlap_fraction`. [Spec: VCEG-M33 | OVT: chapter 9 §9.8.4; chapter 6 §6.5]
- **BD-rate interpolator** — the curve model used to integrate RD curves: classic cubic polynomial (VCEG-M33), PCHIP (JCT-VC/JVET and AOM CTC; OVT default) or Akima. `Interpolator` enum. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.8.4]
- **BdRateResult** — the `ovt_metrics.fbs` table holding a Bjøntegaard comparison: anchor/test point sets, interpolator, `bd_rate_pct`, `bd_metric`, overlap range and warnings. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.5; chapter 9 §9.8.4]
- **Bi-prediction** — inter prediction from two reference blocks (one per list) averaged or weighted; `MotionVector.flags` bit 0; HEVC `inter_pred_idc = PRED_BI` (`HevcInterPredIdc`); AV1 compound prediction (`Av1CompoundType`). [Spec: H.264 §8.4.2.3; H.265 §8.5.3.3.4; AV1 §7.11.3.1]
- **Bit depth** — bits per sample, 8–16 in OVT (`FrameInfo.bit_depth_luma`, `bit_depth_chroma`); H.264 High 10 / HEVC Main 10 / AV1 Main allow 10, AV1 Professional 12. [Spec: H.264 §7.4.2.1.1; H.265 §7.4.3.2.1; AV1 §5.5.2]
- **BitDepthPolicy** — enum in `NormalizationConfig` choosing how mismatched bit depths are reconciled (promote to the higher depth, or to a fixed depth). [OVT: chapter 6 §6.5; chapter 9 §9.2.4]
- **Bit-exact** — two implementations produce identical sample values (or identical serialised bytes) for the same input; required of all OVT software decode paths (vs. the reference decoder) and of the libvmaf host (vs. the `vmaf` CLI). [OVT: chapter 14 §14.5, §14.4.11]
- **BitReader** (`ovt::BitReader`) — the single bounds-checked bit reader through which every OVT parser reads; implements all spec descriptors and throws `ovt::ParseError` on overrun (mapped to `OVT_E_CORRUPT` at module boundaries). [OVT: chapter 7 §7.2]
- **Bits-normalised statistic** — a histogram value expressed as the percentage of the frame's total bits consumed by the counted blocks. [OVT: chapter 4 FR-BLK-020, FR-GUI-031; chapter 11 §11.3.12]
- **BitSpan** — `struct BitSpan { bit_offset:ulong; bit_length:uint; }`: a bit range relative to the RBSP of a NAL unit (NAL header included) or to the first bit of an OBU header; raw-byte positions follow via `rbsp_bit_to_raw_bit` (chapter 7 §7.2.5). `SyntaxNode.span`, `Diagnostic.span`. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.2.1, §7.2.5; chapter 6 §6.2.7]
- **bits per block (estimated)** — see `bits_x8`: entropy-decoder cost in 1/8-bit units; CABAC and range coders have no per-block byte boundary, so only slice/tile/OBU sizes are exact. [OVT: chapter 0 §0.9; chapter 6 §6.3.4]
- **bits_x8** — an entropy-coder cost in 1/8-bit units (libaom accounting resolution): `CellGrid.bits_x8` (per cell, block cost divided by cell count), `Block.bits_x8` (block incl. children), `SymbolCost.bits_x8`. Estimated except for CAVLC; exact bit lengths exist only at partition level (`PartitionInfo.bit_count`). [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.4; chapter 7 §7.6.1]
- **Bjøntegaard metric** (BD-rate, BD-PSNR) — the average bit-rate (or quality) difference between two rate–distortion curves over their common range, computed with piecewise-cubic (JCTVC-L1100), PCHIP or Akima interpolation; OVT matches the FAU-LMS `bjontegaard` package. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.8.4; chapter 4 FR-VQ-017]
- **BLA** (broken link access) — an HEVC IRAP picture type (`BLA_W_LP`, `BLA_W_RADL`, `BLA_N_LP`) marking a splice point; its RASL pictures are not decodable. `FrameInfo.is_rap = true`. [Spec: H.265 §3.11, §8.1.3]
- **Blob id** — the string key of a plane or map in the `PictureStore`: `<source_hash>/<decode_index>/<stage>/<plane>`, with stage tokens `recon`, `pred`, `resid`, `predeblock`, `presaocdef`, `prelr`, `coeff`, `map-<metric_id>`, `tb-<Block.id>` and plane `y`/`u`/`v`/`a`. `PlaneRef.blob_id`, `MapBlob.blob_id`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.6.2]
- **Block** — OVT's codec-neutral word for any coding unit: an H.264 macroblock or sub-macroblock partition, an HEVC CTU/CU/PU/TU, an AV1 superblock/block/transform block. Schema `table Block` with `kind:BlockKind`, `rect`, `level`, `pred`, `qp`, `bits_x8`, `ext`. [OVT: chapter 1 §1.8; chapter 6 §6.3.2]
- **BlockAdditions / BlockAdditionMapping** — Matroska per-block side data and its per-track declaration (e.g. Dolby Vision RPU as `BlockAddIDType`). [Spec: Matroska RFC 9559 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.6.7]
- **BlockKind** — enum `{ MB, SubMB, CTU, CU, PU, TU, SB, Block, TXB, Other }` naming the codec-native type of a `Block`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3]
- **Block map** (metric map) — a per-frame grid of metric values, one per `block_size × block_size` luma samples of the normalised frame, described by `MapBlob` (`U8`/`U16`/`F32` samples) and stored as a blob named `map-<metric_id>`; the on-disk/export form has the 32-byte `OVTMAP01` header. (Chapter 9's glossary calls it a float32 grid; chapter 6's `MapSampleType` also allows U8/U16.) [OVT: chapter 6 §6.5; chapter 9 §9.4.7, §9.9.4]
- **Block Samples** — the GUI panel showing numeric sample grids of the selected block per plane stage (Prediction, Residual, Reconstruction, PreDeblock, PreSaoOrCdef, PreLoopRestoration, Coefficients), one tab per stage and plane. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.3.10]
- **Block tree** — the hierarchy `FrameAnalysis.blocks[]` with `Block.parent`/`children`; `blocks[0]` is the frame root whose children are the top-level CTUs/SBs/MBs in raster order. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.2]
- **Bookmarks & Comments** — the GUI panel listing user bookmarks (frame/block/byte positions) and comments stored in the `.ovtproj` project. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.3.17]
- **Boundary strength** (bS) — the deblocking filter decision per 4-sample edge: H.264 bS 0–4 (4 = intra MB edge), HEVC bS 0–2; AV1 uses filter levels instead. `CellGrid.deblock_bs_v`/`deblock_bs_h` hold the left/top edge value of each cell. [Spec: H.264 §8.7.2.1; H.265 §8.7.2.4 | OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.1]
- **Box** (atom) — the ISOBMFF structural unit: a 4-byte `size` and 4-byte `type` (4CC) header followed by the payload, possibly containing child boxes. `BoxNode` in `ContainerTree`. [Spec: 14496-12 §4.2 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.2.3]
- **BoxContext** — the state (`moov`/`moof`/`meta`/track handler type) OVT propagates while walking boxes so that context-sensitive boxes decode correctly. [OVT: chapter 8 §8.2.7]
- **BoxNode** — the FlatBuffer table for one parsed box: offset, size, header size, type, decoded fields (`Field`), children, diagnostics. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.4; chapter 8 §8.2.5]
- **Brand** — a 4CC in `ftyp`/`styp` (`major_brand` plus `compatible_brands`) declaring conformance to a specification profile (`isom`, `mp42`, `avc1`, `hev1`, `cmfc`, `miaf`, `avif`). [Spec: 14496-12 §4.3 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.4.5]
- **Breakpoint** (layout) — a smallest-width threshold (600, 840, 1200 dp) at which the Android/ChromeOS layout changes panel arrangement; chapter 4 groups these into three *width classes* (< 840, 840–1199, ≥ 1200 dp). [OVT: chapter 11 §11.5.2]
- **BSL-1.0 vs BUSL-1.1** — Boost Software License (permissive, allowed) versus Business Source License (source-available, forbidden); the licence gate distinguishes them by exact SPDX id. [OVT: chapter 15 §15.4]
- **B slice / B frame** — a bi-predictive slice: blocks may use up to two reference pictures (lists L0 and L1) averaged or weighted. `FrameType.B`; `PartitionInfo.slice_type`. [Spec: H.264 §7.4.3; H.265 §7.4.7.1]
- **BT.1886** — the SDR reference display EOTF (gamma 2.4 with black-level lift); display-model preset `bt1886_100` (100 cd/m² peak). [Spec: ITU-R BT.1886 | OVT: chapter 9 §9.2.5]
- **BT.2020** (Rec. 2020) — the UHDTV colour primaries and YCbCr matrix (H.273 `colour_primaries = 9`, `matrix_coeffs = 9/10`); the container of HDR10/HLG content. [Spec: ITU-R BT.2020; H.273 Tables 2, 4]
- **BT.2100** — the HDR television standard defining PQ and HLG, the ICtCp representation and the reference OOTFs. [Spec: ITU-R BT.2100]
- **BT.2390 EETF** — the ITU-R tone-mapping curve (electrical-electrical transfer function) with knee and roll-off; `ToneMap.BT2390`. [Spec: ITU-R BT.2390 §5.4 | OVT: chapter 10 §10.3.4]
- **BT.2408** — the ITU-R report on HDR operational practice that places SDR/graphics reference white at 203 cd/m² (58 % PQ, 75 % HLG). [Spec: ITU-R BT.2408 | OVT: chapter 10 §10.3.2]
- **BT.2446 Method A** — an ITU-R HDR→SDR tone-mapping method; `ToneMap.BT2446A`. [Spec: ITU-R BT.2446 | OVT: chapter 10 §10.3.4]
- **BT.709** (Rec. 709) — the HDTV primaries, OETF and YCbCr matrix (H.273 code 1); the default SDR colour space. [Spec: ITU-R BT.709]
- **Budget file** (`tests/perf/budgets.json`) — the single machine-readable source of every performance and memory budget (chapter 0 §0.7.4), read by `bench_*` benchmarks and the nightly perf job. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.9.3]
- **buffering_period / pic_timing SEI** — the HRD-related SEI messages carrying CPB removal and DPB output delays per picture. [Spec: H.264 §D.1.2–D.1.3; H.265 §D.2.2–D.2.3 | OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.4–7.3.5]
- **Buffer protocol** — CPython's mechanism (`PyObject_GetBuffer`) for exposing raw memory so numpy can create array views without copying; OVT's Python module exposes planes and `CellGrid` vectors this way. [OVT: chapter 12 §12.4.2]
- **Byte service** (page cache) — the core API (`ovt_container_bytes`, `ovt_container_box_at`) through which the hex view, field highlighting and Python read file bytes; backed by a 64 × 64 KiB LRU page cache. [OVT: chapter 8 §8.8]

## C

- **CABAC** (context-adaptive binary arithmetic coding) — the arithmetic entropy coder of H.264 Main+ profiles and all of HEVC; symbols have no byte boundary, so per-block bit costs are estimates. `PartitionInfo.cabac_init`. [Spec: H.264 §9.3; H.265 §9.3]
- **cabac_zero_words** — padding `0x0000` words appended after the slice data of an H.264/HEVC CABAC slice to satisfy the bin-to-bit ratio constraint; counted in `size_bytes`, excluded from `bits_x8`. [Spec: H.264 §7.4.2.10; H.265 §7.4.9.4]
- **Cache entry** — the directory `<cache_root>/<source_hash>/` holding `meta.json`, `index.fb`, `frames/`, `planes/`, `maps/`, `metrics/`; FlatBuffers inside are size-prefixed and zstd-compressed. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.7.1]
- **CAMBI** (Contrast-Aware Multiscale Banding Index) — Netflix's banding detector in libvmaf (0 = no banding, higher = worse); OVT id `cambi`, full- or no-reference mode, optional heatmap. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.5.3]
- **Canonical FlatBuffer** — a buffer written by the canonical builder (fixed field order, no shared strings, zero-filled padding) so that identical content gives identical bytes; required for determinism tests and cache keys. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.11; chapter 0 §0.7 rule 5]
- **Canonical JSON family** — one of the fixed JSON shapes (`syntax-elements`, `header-struct`, `block-grid`, `box-tree`, `sample-table`, `scores`) into which oracle and OVT outputs are normalised before a golden comparison. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.4.0]
- **Canonical order / canonicalisation** — the fixed ordering of every vector and the schema-order rebuild of a buffer that make identical analyses byte-identical (rules V-025, V-032). [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.7, §6.9]
- **Catch2** — the C++ unit-test framework (v3, BSL-1.0) used for `core/tests/`, `render/tests/` and `cli/tests/`; test names in acceptance lines (`test:<Name>`) are Catch2 test cases. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.2]
- **CAVLC** (context-adaptive variable-length coding) — H.264's table-based entropy coder (Baseline profile); the only coder for which per-block bit counts are exact. [Spec: H.264 §9.2]
- **cbcs / cenc / cbc1 / cens** — the four CENC protection schemes: AES-CTR full-sample (`cenc`), AES-CBC full-sample (`cbc1`), AES-CTR pattern (`cens`), AES-CBC pattern with constant IV (`cbcs`, used by HLS/FairPlay). `EncryptionInfo.scheme_type`. [Spec: 23001-7 §10 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.3 group H]
- **cbf** (coded block flag) — whether a transform block has any non-zero coefficient; `CellGrid.cbf` bit0 = Y, bit1 = Cb, bit2 = Cr. [Spec: H.264 §7.4.5 (`coded_block_pattern`); H.265 §7.4.9.8; AV1 `all_zero` §5.11.39]
- **CBS** (Coded Bitstream framework) — FFmpeg's internal header parser (`libavcodec/cbs_*.c`) whose `trace_headers` BSF output is OVT's syntax oracle. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.7.1]
- **CDEF** (constrained directional enhancement filter) — AV1's in-loop deringing filter applied per 8×8 with a direction search; strengths are selected per 64×64 via `cdef_idx` (0–7) into a frame table of up to 8 (primary, secondary) strength pairs. `CellGrid.filter_idx`, `Av1CdefParams`. [Spec: AV1 §5.9.19, §7.15 | OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.1]
- **CDEF strength** — the per-frame table entries `cdef_y_pri_strength`/`cdef_y_sec_strength` (0–15 / 0–4) and the UV equivalents, plus `cdef_damping` (3–6). [Spec: AV1 §5.9.19]
- **Cell** — OVT's smallest addressable analysis unit: a 4×4 luma-sample square in coded-picture coordinates (H.264 MV granularity, HEVC minimum TB, AV1 MI unit). Cells form the `CellGrid`, indexed `y4 * cells_w + x4`; `struct CellIndex`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.1 P2]
- **Cell CSV** — `ovt analyze -f csv`: one row per cell with the `CellGrid` columns. [OVT: chapter 12 §12.2.8]
- **CellGrid** — the structure-of-arrays table holding per-cell analysis values (`block_id`, `partition_id`, `pred`, `qp`, `intra_mode_y/c`, `mv0_x`/`mv0_y`, `mv1_x`/`mv1_y`, `ref0`/`ref1`, `tx_log2_w`/`tx_log2_h`, `tx_type`, `cbf`, `bits_x8`, `deblock_bs_v/h`, `filter_idx`); every vector has `cells_w × cells_h` entries or is empty (not provided). [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3, §6.3.1]
- **CENC** (Common Encryption) — ISO/IEC 23001-7: boxes `schm`/`tenc`/`senc`/`saiz`/`saio`/`pssh`, schemes `cenc`, `cbc1`, `cens`, `cbcs`; OVT decodes the metadata and shades encrypted byte ranges but never decrypts. `EncryptionInfo`, `SampleEncryptionInfo`, `SubsampleEncryption`. [Spec: 23001-7 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.4.4]
- **CfL** (chroma from luma) — AV1 intra chroma mode predicting Cb/Cr as a scaled version of the reconstructed luma AC plus DC (`UV_CFL_PRED`, alphas `cfl_alpha_u/v`). [Spec: AV1 §5.11.45, §7.11.5 | OVT: chapter 6 `Av1Block`]
- **ChromaArrayType** — H.264/HEVC derived variable: 0 for monochrome or `separate_colour_plane_flag = 1`, else `chroma_format_idc`; decides whether chroma syntax is present. [Spec: H.264 §7.4.2.1.1; H.265 §7.4.3.2.1]
- **Chroma format** — the subsampling of Cb/Cr relative to Y: 4:0:0 (mono), 4:2:0 (half in both axes), 4:2:2, 4:4:4. `FrameInfo.chroma_format` 0–3; H.273 `chroma_format_idc`. [Spec: H.264 §6.2; H.265 §6.2; AV1 §6.4.2]
- **Chroma siting** (chroma siting) — the horizontal/vertical position of chroma samples relative to luma (left/centre; top-left for BT.2020 UHD; H.273 `chroma_sample_loc_type` 0–5); zimg needs it for correct upsampling. [Spec: H.273 §8.7 | OVT: chapter 9 §9.2.4]
- **Chunk** (ISOBMFF) — a run of consecutive samples of one track stored contiguously; `stsc` maps samples to chunks, `stco`/`co64` give chunk file offsets. [Spec: 14496-12 §8.7.4–8.7.5 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.4.1]
- **cICP chunk** — PNG (Third Edition) chunk carrying H.273 primaries/transfer/matrix/full-range codes; OVT writes it in HDR screenshots. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.8]
- **CIEDE2000** — the CIE 2000 colour-difference formula (ΔE00) in CIELAB; libvmaf feature `ciede`, OVT id `ciede2000`. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.5.3]
- **CIE xy** — chromaticity coordinates from the CIE 1931 2° observer (`x = X/(X+Y+Z)`, `y = Y/(X+Y+Z)`), range 0–0.8; OVT's CIE xy scope plots sample chromaticities against the BT.709/P3/BT.2020 triangles. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.7.7]
- **CLI11** — the C++ command-line parsing library (BSD-3-Clause) used by `ovt`. [OVT: chapter 12 §12.2]
- **Click-cycling** — repeated left-clicks at the same canvas position select successively smaller enclosing blocks along the codec's hierarchy chain (UX-4). [OVT: chapter 3 §3.4; chapter 11 §11.4.1]
- **CliEnvelope** — see *Envelope (CLI)*; the field list is owned by chapter 12 §12.1.4 and is not repeated here. [OVT: chapter 12 §12.1.4]
- **CLL** (content light level; MaxCLL/MaxFALL) — see *MaxCLL*, *MaxFALL*; carried in the `clli` box, the HEVC `content_light_level_info` SEI (type 144) and the AV1 `METADATA_TYPE_HDR_CLL` OBU. `ovt_ColorDescription.max_cll`/`max_fall` (the image carried in `HdrMetadata.color`). [Spec: CTA-861.3; H.265 §D.3.35; AV1 §5.8.4]
- **CMAF** (Common Media Application Format) — ISO/IEC 23000-19 constraints on fragmented ISOBMFF for DASH/HLS delivery; structural brands `cmfc`, `cmf2`, `cmfs`, `cmff`, `cmfl` (track, 2nd-edition track, segment, fragment, chunk). [Spec: 23000-19 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.4.5]
- **Codec extension table** — the per-codec FlatBuffer table (`AvcMb`, `AvcSubMb`, `HevcCtu`, `HevcCu`, `HevcPu`, `HevcTu`, `Av1Block`, `Av1Txb`) stored as a nested buffer in `Block.ext` and named by `Block.ext_type`, holding every syntax element read for that block. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.3]
- **Codec pack** — the separately distributed set of AVC/HEVC decoder plugins (`libovt_plugin_ffmpeg`, `libovt_plugin_de265`) with `PATENT_NOTICE.md`, installed on demand (Play Feature Delivery on Android) per ADR-015. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.4.8; chapter 15 §15.5]
- **codec_private** — the opaque decoder configuration (`avcC`/`hvcC`/`av1C` payload) extracted from the container and passed to a plugin's `create()`. [OVT: chapter 8 §8.7.6; chapter 0 §0.6.2]
- **Coded picture** — one decoded frame or field as a unit of decoding; one `FrameInfo` per coded picture (plus one per AV1 `show_existing_frame` header). [Spec: H.264 §3.30; H.265 §3.26 | OVT: chapter 6 §6.2.5]
- **Coded picture coordinates** — luma-sample coordinates of the decoded picture before cropping (H.264 frame cropping, HEVC conformance window, AV1 render size), origin top-left; all `Rect`s in the model use them. `FrameInfo.coded_size` vs `crop`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.2.1]
- **Code value** (domain) — the integer sample values as stored in the stream (Y'CbCr, 8–16 bit); the default metric working domain (`WorkingDomain.CodeValue`, CLI id `code_value`). [OVT: chapter 9 §9.2.5]
- **Coefficients** (`PlaneStage.Coefficients`) — the dequantised (or raw, per plugin caps) transform coefficients of each transform block, exported as a plane stage or per-TB blob `tb-<Block.id>` (`HevcTu.coeff_blob_id`, `Av1Txb.coeff_blob_id`). [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.6, §6.6.2]
- **ColorVideoVDP** — a GPU-based full-reference video quality metric (Mantiuk et al.) with a display model; a phase-4 sidecar candidate, not an OVT kernel. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.1.3]
- **CompareMode** — `libovt-render` enum for A/B presentation: side-by-side, split/wipe, difference, flicker/toggle. `VideoSurface::set_compare()`. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.2.4]
- **Comparison project** — a GUI session holding one reference and up to eight distorted files for joint metric runs and RD plotting; stored in `.ovtproj`. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.2.6, §11.3.13]
- **Composition offset** (`CTS − DTS`) — the per-sample signed offset stored in `ctts` or `trun.sample_composition_time_offset` that turns decode time into composition time. [Spec: 14496-12 §8.6.1.3 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.4.1]
- **Composition-to-DTS shift** — the constant (`cslg.compositionToDTSShift`) added to all CTS so that CTS ≥ DTS for every sample. [Spec: 14496-12 §8.6.1.4]
- **Conformance bitstream** — a stream published by a standards body (JVT, JCT-VC, AOM/Argon) exercising specific syntax features and shipping with reference decoded output hashes. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.3.1]
- **Conformance window** — the HEVC cropping rectangle (`conf_win_*_offset` in chroma units) defining the output picture inside the coded picture; H.264 equivalent is `frame_crop_*_offset`, AV1's is `render_width`/`render_height`. `FrameInfo.crop`. [Spec: H.265 §7.4.3.2.1; H.264 §7.4.2.1.1 | OVT: chapter 6 §6.2.1]
- **ContainerRoot** — the root table (file identifier `OVTC`) of the container FlatBuffer: tree, tracks and the per-track derived views (`sample_tables[]`, `edit_lists[]`, `fragment_timelines[]`, `encryption[]`, `hdr[]`, keyed by `track_id`) plus `raw_format` for raw YUV/Y4M inputs. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.4]
- **Container tree** — the GUI panel and `ContainerTree`/`BoxNode` FlatBuffer presenting the parsed box hierarchy with offsets, lazily expanded via `ovt_container_expand`. [OVT: chapter 8 §8.2.4; chapter 11 §11.3.7]
- **Content URI** — an Android `content://` resource handle obtained through the Storage Access Framework and opened as a file descriptor (`ovt_file_open_fd`). [OVT: chapter 7 §7.1.3; chapter 11 §11.5.6]
- **Convex hull** (encoding ladder) — the upper convex hull of several RD curves (one per resolution) in the `(log10 rate, metric)` plane, computed with Andrew's monotone chain; `ovt bdrate --hull`. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.8.5]
- **Corpus id** — the stable identifier `<source>_<name>` of a test media file in `tests/corpora/manifest.json`; `<source>` is one of the manifest source ids `jvt`, `jctvc`, `aom`, `argon`, `mpegff`, `gpac`, `bento4`, `dashif`, `cta`, `apple`, `netflix`, `ytugc`, `hdr`, `heif`, `ts`, `syn`. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.2.4]
- **Corresponding Source** — the GPL §6 term for the complete source and build scripts needed to reproduce a binary; OVT publishes it as an archive with each release. [OVT: chapter 15 §15.8]
- **CPB** (coded picture buffer) — the HRD's bitstream input buffer whose fill level the `buffering_period`/`pic_timing` delays describe; its size `CpbSize[SchedSelIdx]` (bits) is derived from `cpb_size_value_minus1` and `cpb_size_scale` of `hrd_parameters()` (stored as `cpb_size_bits`). [Spec: H.264 Annex C, §E.2.2; H.265 Annex C, §E.3.3]
- **CpbSize** — the CPB size in bits for one `SchedSelIdx`, derived from `hrd_parameters()`; see *CPB*. [Spec: H.264 §E.2.2; H.265 §E.3.3]
- **CRA** (clean random access) — an HEVC IRAP type after which leading pictures (RASL) may reference earlier pictures; when decoding starts at a CRA its RASL pictures are dropped (`display_index = -1`). [Spec: H.265 §3.25, §8.1.3]
- **Critical path** — the longest chain of dependent work items, measured in milestone ordinals and WI sizes, that ends at a phase gate; any slip on it slips the gate. [OVT: chapter 16 §16.5]
- **Crop** — see *conformance window*; `FrameInfo.crop` is the output rectangle inside `coded_size`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.2.1]
- **CTB vs CTU** — in HEVC a *coding tree block* is the luma (or one chroma) sample array of size `CtbSizeY` (16/32/64); a *coding tree unit* is the luma CTB plus its two chroma CTBs and syntax. OVT's `BlockKind.CTU` is the unit; `HevcCtu` is its extension table. [Spec: H.265 §3.33–3.34]
- **CTS** (composition time) — the presentation timestamp of a sample in the track timescale; `FrameInfo.pts` after edit-list mapping. [Spec: 14496-12 §8.6.1 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.4.2]
- **CU** (coding unit) — the HEVC leaf of the CTU quadtree (8×8 to 64×64) that carries the prediction mode and partitioning; `BlockKind.CU`, `HevcCu`, `HevcPredMode`. [Spec: H.265 §7.3.8.5]
- **cu_qp_delta** — HEVC's per-quantization-group QP change; the final QP is `CellGrid.qp`. [Spec: H.265 §7.4.9.14, §8.6.1]
- **CVS** (coded video sequence) — the run of access units from one IRAP/IDR with `NoRaslOutputFlag = 1` up to the next; see *minimum CVS*. [Spec: H.264 §3.30; H.265 §3.29]
- **Cycle level** — the position in the codec's block hierarchy chain (MB→SubMB→Tx; CTU→CU→PU→TU; SB→Block→TXB) that repeated clicks or `Tab` move through. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.4.1]

## D

## E

- **Eligible WI** — a work item whose dependencies are all Done, whose milestone is the current or an earlier one of its phase, which is not superseded and not assigned to anyone (chapter 16 §16.2.1). [OVT: chapter 16 §16.2.1]

[OVT: chapter 10 §10.1.2]
- **Emulation prevention byte** (EPB) — the `0x03` inserted in H.26x NAL payloads after `00 00` to avoid false start codes; removed when forming the RBSP. `Unit.epb_positions` records raw byte offsets of removed bytes so RBSP bit positions map back to file bytes. - **Encoding toolset summary** — a per-stream list of coding tools used at least once, with frame and block counts (Stream Info panel). [OVT: chapter 4 FR-BLK-021; chapter 11 §11.3.8]

[OVT: chapter 9 §9.8.1; chapter 6 §6.5]
- **PQ** (perceptual quantizer, ST 2084) — the absolute HDR EOTF (H.273 transfer 16) mapping a 0–1 signal to 0–10 000 cd/m². [Spec: SMPTE ST 2084; ITU-R BT.2100 Table 4]
- **Prediction / Residual** (plane stages) — the predicted samples before adding the residual, and the decoded residual (signed) itself; `Reconstruction = Prediction + Residual` before in-loop filters. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.6]

- **Primary benchmark** — one of the two commercial products (ViCueSoft VQ Analyzer, Elecard StreamEye Studio) whose feature areas OVT's coverage is measured against. [OVT: chapter 3 §3.1]

- **P slice / P frame** — a predictive slice using one reference per block (list L0). `FrameType.P`. [Spec: H.264 §7.4.3; H.265 §7.4.7.1]
- **PSNR-HVS / PSNR-HVS-M** — DCT-domain PSNR weighted by a contrast-sensitivity function (-M adds masking), from Daala tools; libvmaf feature `psnr_hvs`. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.5.3]
- **PSS** — Proportional Set Size, Android's per-process memory measure (`dumpsys meminfo`) used for the 1 GB budget. [OVT: chapter 4 NFR; chapter 14 §14.9.3]
- **pssh** — the Protection System Specific Header box carrying DRM system ids and initialisation data; OVT lists system ids in `EncryptionInfo` but never decrypts. [Spec: ISO/IEC 23001-7 §8.1 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.4.4]
- **pssh / SystemID** — the Protection System Specific Header box carrying DRM initialisation data for a 16-byte system UUID (Widevine, PlayReady, FairPlay); `PsshInfo`. [Spec: 23001-7 §8.1]

- **PU** (prediction unit) — the HEVC partition of a CU that carries one prediction (intra mode or MVs); `BlockKind.PU`, `HevcPu`. [Spec: H.265 §7.3.8.6]
- **PU21** — Mantiuk & Azimi's perceptually uniform encoding of absolute luminance (2021; `banding_glare` variant), enabling PU-PSNR/PU-SSIM on HDR content; `WorkingDomain.PU21`. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.2.5]

## Q

- **QP / qindex** — the quantisation parameter: H.264/HEVC QP 0–51 for 8-bit (range extended by −6 per extra bit; step doubles every 6), AV1 `qindex` 0–255 (`base_q_idx` plus deltas). `CellGrid.qp` holds the final luma value per cell; `FrameInfo.qp_base`; `FrameAnalysis.qp_hist` (256 bins). [Spec: H.264 §7.4.2.2, §8.6.1; H.265 §8.6.1; AV1 §7.12.2]
- **QQuickRhiItem** — Qt 6.7+ Quick item rendering through QRhi into a texture composited by the scene graph; the base of `PictureHost`. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.4.2]
- **QtQuickView** — Qt's Android view embedding a Qt Quick scene inside a conventional `Activity`; OVT's Android shell uses it instead of a Qt-owned activity so that `SurfaceView` HDR output works. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.4; chapter 0 ADR-004]
- **Quantization group** — the HEVC square region (size `Log2MinCuQpDeltaSize`) within which one `cu_qp_delta` applies (QP derivation H.265 §8.6.1). [Spec: H.265 §7.4.9.14, §8.6.1]
- **QuickTime quirks** — `.mov` deviations OVT handles: `wide` placeholder, `nclc` colour, `meta` without version/flags, Pascal strings in `hdlr`, `wave`/`enda`/`chan` audio extensions, `tapt` apertures, `gmhd`. [OVT: chapter 8 §8.3 group M]

## R

- **RADL / RASL** — HEVC leading pictures (output before their IRAP in display order): RADL are decodable from the IRAP, RASL reference earlier pictures and are skipped after random access. [Spec: H.265 §3.112–3.113]
- **Random access point** (RAP) — a picture at which decoding can start: H.264 IDR (or recovery point), HEVC IRAP, AV1 keyframe. `FrameInfo.is_rap`; the index keeps `rap_before[d]`. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.4.2]
- **Range coder** — AV1's multi-symbol adaptive arithmetic coder (15-bit CDFs); like CABAC it has no per-block byte boundary. [Spec: AV1 §8.2]
- **RawVideoFormat** — the parsed form of the raw YUV grammar `WxH:pix_fmt[:fps_num/fps_den]` (geometry, FFmpeg pixel format, frame rate, start offset) used by `ovt_container_open_format` and the Open Raw dialog. [OVT: chapter 8 §8.6.6; chapter 12 §12.1.5]
- **RBSP / SODB** — raw byte sequence payload: the NAL payload after EPB removal, ending with `rbsp_stop_one_bit` and alignment zeros; the string of data bits (SODB) is the RBSP without the trailing bits. OVT bit positions are RBSP-relative. [Spec: H.264 §7.3.2.11; H.265 §7.3.2.12 | OVT: chapter 7 §7.2.5]
- **RD curve** (rate–distortion curve) — a series of `RdPoint { bitrate_kbps, quality, label }` for one encoder configuration, sorted by bitrate (`BdRateResult.anchor`/`test`). [OVT: chapter 9 §9.8.4]
- **Reconstruction** — the final decoded picture after all in-loop filters; `PlaneStage.Reconstruction`, blob stage `recon`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.6]
- **Recording mode** — `SyntaxBuilder::set_recording`: whether reads create `SyntaxNode`s or only return values. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.2]
- **Reduced WI / Superseded WI** — a work item implemented with a smaller scope than its chapter block states (reduced), or not implemented because a primary WI delivers its content (superseded); both are fixed in chapter 16 §16.3. [OVT: chapter 16 §16.3]
- **Reference hardware** — the fixed machines on which performance budgets are normative (`m1-air-8gb`, `linux-x64-ref`, `pixel-tablet`, `chromebook-hdr`). [OVT: chapter 14 §14.9.2]
- **Reference list** (L0/L1, RefPicList0/1) — the ordered lists of reference pictures a P or B slice may index (`ref_idx_l0/l1`); `PartitionInfo.num_ref_idx_active`, `MotionVector.list`. AV1 instead names 7 references (`LAST`…`ALTREF`, `Av1RefFrame`). [Spec: H.264 §8.2.4; H.265 §8.3.4; AV1 §6.10.24]
- **RefSlot** — `FrameAnalysis.refs[]` entry describing one reference actually usable by the frame's blocks (decode index, POC/order hint, name such as `L0[0]`/`LAST`); `refs[]` is indexed by *reference type* with INTRA at index 0, and `RefSlot.slot` (−1 = n/a) carries the DPB / AV1 `ref_frame_idx` slot (D6). `CellGrid.ref0/ref1` index `refs[]`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3]
- **Regression input** — a minimised fuzz crash input committed under `core/tests/fuzz/regressions/` (D14) and replayed on every platform. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.7.5]
- **Reproducible build** — a build whose output is byte-identical given the same source and toolchain (`SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`); required for F-Droid verification. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.8]
- **Result<T>** (`ovt::Result<T>`) — the core's value-or-status return type (`std::expected`-like, carrying `ovt_status` and an optional `Diagnostic`) used inside C++ modules and unwrapped at the C API; declared in `core/include/ovt/cxx/result.hpp` (D22). [OVT: chapter 5 §5.6; chapter 12 §12.3.1]
- **REUSE** — the FSFE specification for machine-readable licensing (SPDX headers, `LICENSES/`, `REUSE.toml`); `reuse lint` runs in CI. [OVT: chapter 15 §15.1]
- **RHI** (Qt Rendering Hardware Interface) — Qt's abstraction over Vulkan/Metal/D3D/OpenGL; `QQuickRhiItem` hosts the EMBEDDED texture. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.1.2]
- **Role** (A/B, reference/distorted) — the function of an open file in a project: inspected stream A, comparison partner B, metric reference/distorted. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.2.6]
- **row-MT** — row-based multithreading of libaom/libde265 decoders; disabled in analysis builds where it would change accounting order. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.6]
- **RPS** (reference picture set) — HEVC's explicit per-picture list of retained references in five subsets (StCurrBefore, StCurrAfter, StFoll, LtCurr, LtFoll), derived from `st_ref_pic_set` and long-term entries; `HevcRps`. [Spec: H.265 §7.3.7, §8.3.2]
- **RPU** (reference processing unit) — Dolby Vision per-frame metadata (NAL type 62 in HEVC, Matroska BlockAddition) describing reshaping polynomials/MMR and L1–L11 levels; OVT parses and displays it. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.11; chapter 10 §10.3.7]

## S

- **SAF** (Storage Access Framework) — Android's document-provider API for opening and saving files by `content://` URI through the system picker; OVT opens the returned descriptor with `ovt_file_open_fd`. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.5.6; chapter 13 §13.4]
- **Sample** (ISOBMFF) — the unit of media addressed by the sample tables: one coded frame / access unit / temporal unit (video) or one audio frame; `Unit.sample_index` links units to samples. [Spec: 14496-12 §3.1, §8.5 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.4.1]

[Spec: 14496-12 §8.5.2 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.3 group B]

- **Scalable layers** (temporal / spatial) — sub-streams selectable by `temporal_id` (frame-rate scalability) and `layer_id`/`spatial_id` (resolution or view); `Unit.temporal_id`, `Unit.layer_id`, `FrameInfo.temporal_id`/`layer_id`. [Spec: H.264 Annex G; H.265 §7.4.2.2, Annex F; AV1 §5.3.3]

- **scdet** — FFmpeg's scene-change detection filter whose SAD-based score and threshold (default 10.0) OVT reproduces for `ovt scenes` and per-scene pooling. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.8.2; chapter 4 FR-VQ-021]
- **Scene cut / scene segment** — a shot boundary detected where `min(mafd, |Δmafd|) ≥ 10` on the 8-bit scale (FFmpeg `scdet`-equivalent); a *scene segment* (`SceneSegment`) is the run of display frames between cuts, used for per-scene pooling. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.8.2]
- **Scene graph** (Qt Quick) — Qt's retained render tree; OVT's picture item is a textured node fed by `VideoSurface`, overlays are rendered inside the surface, never as QML items. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.2.3]
- **Schema major** (`schema_version`) — the compatibility generation of a FlatBuffers schema (= 1); readers of major *n* read every buffer of major *n*; increments only on breaking changes. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.1]
- **scRGB** (extended linear sRGB) — linear-light RGB with BT.709 primaries where 1.0 = SDR white and values > 1.0 are HDR; the EDR layer format. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.4.1]
- **SDF atlas** — the signed-distance-field glyph texture (`render/fonts/`) used for overlay labels at any zoom without re-rasterising. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.5.5]
- **SDF glyphs** (signed-distance-field atlas) — a texture storing per-pixel distance to the glyph edge so text stays crisp at any zoom from one 1024×1024 R8 atlas (DejaVu Sans Mono). [OVT: chapter 10 §10.5.1]
- **SDR / SDR white** (reference white) — standard dynamic range (BT.709/BT.1886, ≤ 100 nits nominal); SDR white is the luminance at which UI and SDR content white are shown in an HDR composition: 203 cd/m² per BT.2408, `PL_COLOR_SDR_WHITE`, `DisplayTarget.sdr_white_nits`. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.3.2]
- **Seed corpus** — the initial inputs a fuzz target starts from; OVT seeds are real units extracted from the corpora, minimised with `-merge=1`, and committed under `core/tests/fuzz/corpus/<name>/`. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.7.2]
- **Segmentation** (AV1) — up to 8 segments per frame, each with feature deltas (`qindex`, loop-filter levels, reference, skip, global MV), assigned per block via `segment_id`. [Spec: AV1 §5.9.14, §5.11.9]

- **SEI** (supplemental enhancement information) — H.26x NAL units (H.264 type 6; HEVC prefix 39 / suffix 40) carrying non-normative messages: HDR metadata, timing, picture hash, user data (T.35, unregistered). `StreamIndex.sei`. [Spec: H.264 Annex D; H.265 Annex D]

- **Semi-public API** — (a) in OVT: the C++ helper headers under `core/include/ovt/cxx/*.hpp` (`result.hpp`, `jobs.hpp`, `log.hpp`, `cancel.hpp`; D22) that the GUI, CLI and tests may use but that carry no ABI promise, unlike the C API; (b) of a third-party library: a header that is part of its source tree but not installed (libaom's `av1/decoder/inspection.h`), which OVT includes from the pinned source tree, never from a vendored copy (chapter 5 §5.2.2). - **Sidecar** — (1) *metric sidecar*: a metric implemented as an external process speaking the OVT JSON Lines protocol over stdio with frames in shared memory (Python research metrics); (2) *raw-YUV sidecar*: the `.ovt-raw.json` file next to a raw YUV file declaring its format. - **Sub-macroblock** — an H.264 8×8 partition of a `P_8x8`/`B_8x8` macroblock with its own `sub_mb_type` (8×8, 8×4, 4×8, 4×4); `BlockKind.SubMB`, `AvcSubMb`, `AvcSubMbType`. [Spec: H.264 §7.4.5.2]
- **Subsample encryption** — CENC per-sample map of clear/encrypted byte ranges (`senc`/`saiz`/`saio`), used to keep NAL headers readable. [Spec: ISO/IEC 23001-7 §7 | OVT: chapter 8 §8.4.4]
- **Substream** (WPP) — the entropy-coded data of one CTU row under HEVC wavefront parallel processing, delimited by entry points; `PartitionKind.Substream`. [Spec: H.265 §7.4.7.1 (`entropy_coding_sync_enabled_flag`)]
- **Superblock** (SB) — AV1's top-level block, 64×64 or 128×128 luma samples (`use_128x128_superblock`), recursively partitioned into blocks down to 4×4; `BlockKind.SB`. [Spec: AV1 §5.11.4, §6.10.4]
- **superres** (AV1 super-resolution) — frame coded at reduced width (`UpscaledWidth` > `FrameWidth`) and upscaled after CDEF; OVT reports both widths and draws overlays in coded coordinates. [Spec: AV1 §7.16 | OVT: chapter 7 §7.3]
- **SurfaceView / TextureView** — Android views for video: `SurfaceView` owns a separate compositor surface (can be HDR-tagged, used by OVT); `TextureView` renders into the view hierarchy (SDR only). [OVT: chapter 10 §10.4.3]
- **SVC** (scalable video coding) — H.264 Annex G temporal/spatial/quality scalability (NAL types 14/20); OVT indexes headers and decodes the base layer. [Spec: H.264 Annex G | OVT: chapter 7 WI-4.111]
- **Switch frame** (AV1) — `frame_type = SWITCH_FRAME`: an inter frame that can be decoded after a stream switch because it uses only slots with fixed semantics; `FrameType.Switch`. [Spec: AV1 §6.8.2]
- **Symbol accounting** — libaom's optional per-symbol bit-cost tracking (`CONFIG_ACCOUNTING`) that attributes 1/8-bit costs to symbols and positions; the source of `SymbolCost`. `ovt_AnalysisCaps.has_symbol_accounting`. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.6.1]
- **SymbolCost** — `table SymbolCost { x4; y4; symbol_id; bits_x8; count }`: per-symbol entropy cost at a 4×4 cell, with names in `FrameAnalysis.symbol_names`; AV1 only: derived from libaom's symbol-cost accounting (`Accounting.syms`, `CONFIG_ACCOUNTING`, see *Symbol accounting*), the only source of per-block bits for AV1 (libaom has no per-superblock bit count, D30). [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.5; chapter 7 §7.6.1]
- **Sync set / sync lock** — in dual view, the set of properties (frame, zoom, pan, plane mode, overlay mode) kept equal between the two surfaces; each can be unlocked individually. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.10; chapter 4 FR-GUI-021]
- **SyntaxBuilder** — the recorder that turns `BitReader` reads into `SyntaxNode` trees (or plain values in non-recording mode). [OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.2]
- **Syntax element** — one named value read from the bitstream by the spec's syntax tables (e.g. `log2_max_pic_order_cnt_lsb_minus4`); `SyntaxNode` records its name, descriptor, value, bit span and spec clause. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.3]
- **SyntaxNode** — the FlatBuffer table for one syntax element or structure: `name`, `descriptor`, `kind:SyntaxValueKind`, value fields, `enum_name`, `derived`, `span:BitSpan`, `spec_ref`, `children`, `array_index`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3]
- **Syntax tree** — the GUI panel (and `SyntaxNode` hierarchy) showing a unit's parsed header fields with values, derived variables and bit positions; clicking highlights bytes in the hex view. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.3.4]
- **Synthetic stream** — a test stream OVT generates from lavfi test patterns with pinned encoders (`syn_*`) to exercise a named codec feature. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.3.3]

## T

- **T.35** — ITU-T T.35 registered user data (country code + provider code) carried in `user_data_registered_itu_t_t35` SEI / AV1 `METADATA_TYPE_ITUT_T35`; dispatches to HDR10+, CEA-708 captions and others. [Spec: H.264 §D.1.6; AV1 §5.8.2 | OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.11]
- **Temporal delimiter** (`OBU_TEMPORAL_DELIMITER`) — the empty OBU starting every AV1 temporal unit; OVT uses it (with `show_existing_frame`) for temporal-unit boundaries. [Spec: AV1 §5.6]
- **Temporal unit** (TU, AV1) — all OBUs from one temporal delimiter to the next, containing exactly one shown frame per spatial layer; the AV1 access unit. [Spec: AV1 §7.5 | OVT: chapter 7 §7.3.9]
- **Test IPC** — the JSON-lines control socket of `ovt-studio --test-ipc` used by scripted interaction tests. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.10.3]
- **Test tag** — a Catch2 tag (`[fast]`, `[slow]`, `[needs-corpus]`, `[needs-gpu]`, `[needs-display]`, `[needs-android]`, `[golden]`, `[fuzz-regress]`) selecting which CI job runs a test; mirrored as a ctest label. [OVT: chapter 14 §14.2.5]
- **tfdt** (`baseMediaDecodeTime`) — the DTS of the first sample of a track fragment. [Spec: 14496-12 §8.8.12]
- **Tile / tile group** — a rectangular region of CTUs (HEVC, `tiles_enabled_flag`, column/row boundaries) or of superblocks (AV1, `tile_cols`/`tile_rows`, up to 64×64 tiles) decodable independently within a frame; AV1 groups tiles into *tile group* OBUs. `PartitionKind.Tile/TileGroup`, `PartitionInfo.tile_row`/`tile_col`. [Spec: H.265 §6.3.1; AV1 §5.9.15, §5.11.1]
- **Timebase** — the rational unit of `pts`/`dts` (`FrameInfo.timebase_num`/`timebase_den`, e.g. 1/90000). [OVT: chapter 6 §6.2.6]
- **Timeline** — the bottom GUI region holding the frame-size bar chart, QP plot and filmstrip; the primary navigation control. [OVT: chapter 11 §11.3.1]
- **ToneMap** — `ovt::render` enum selecting the tone-mapping function (`Auto`, `BT2390`, `BT2446A`, `Spline`, `Clip`, `None`); `Auto` picks BT.2390 when the display peak is below the content peak. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.2.3; chapter 4 FR-HDR-004]
- **Tone mapping** — compressing content luminance into the display's range (`ToneMap { Auto, BT2390, BT2446A, Spline, Clip, None }`); *Clip* is the analysis mode that shows out-of-range samples clipped rather than compressed. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.3.4]
- **TR 101 290** — the ETSI measurement guideline listing MPEG-TS error checks in three priorities; OVT implements priorities 1–2. [OVT: chapter 8 WI-4.207]
- **Trace format / trace text** — the line format `bit_offset name[idx] bits = value` shared by FFmpeg `trace_headers` and `ovt syntax --trace`. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.7.1; chapter 12 §12.2.3]
- **TrackInfo** — the per-track container record: `track_id`, `HandlerType`, codec string (RFC 6381), sample entry fourcc, timescale, duration, dimensions, language, matrix/rotation, bitrates; the derived views (sample table, edit list, fragments, encryption, HDR) are separate tables in `ContainerRoot` keyed by `track_id`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.4; derivation rules chapter 8 §8.1.3]
- **TrackReference** — `tref` entry (`hint`, `cdsc`, `sbas`, `scal`, `tbas`, …) linking a track to others; stored in `TrackInfo.references`. [Spec: ISO/IEC 14496-12 §8.3.3 | OVT: chapter 6 §6.4]
- **Transform skip** — coding a residual block without a transform (HEVC `transform_skip_flag` for 4×4; AV1 `IDTX` identity transform type); `CellGrid.tx_type`. [Spec: H.265 §7.3.8.11, §8.6.4.2; AV1 §5.11.47]
- **Transform type / size** — the inverse transform applied to a block: HEVC DCT-II or 4×4 DST-VII (`tx_type`), sizes 4–32; AV1 16 types from DCT/ADST/flipADST/identity combinations (`Av1TxType`), sizes 4×4 to 64×64 incl. rectangular (`Av1TxSize`). `CellGrid.tx_log2_w`/`tx_log2_h`, `tx_type`. [Spec: H.265 §8.6.4; AV1 §7.13.2]
- **Triplet** (vcpkg) — a vcpkg configuration naming target architecture, OS and linkage, e.g. `arm64-osx-ovt`. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.3.2]
- **TU** (transform unit) — the HEVC leaf of the residual quadtree carrying one transform per plane; `BlockKind.TU`, `HevcTu`. [Spec: H.265 §7.3.8.8]
- **TXB** (transform block, AV1) — one transform of a plane within an AV1 block; `BlockKind.TXB`, `Av1Txb`. [Spec: AV1 §5.11.35]
- **TxSize** — AV1 transform size enumeration (`TX_4X4` … `TX_64X64` and rectangular sizes) recorded per block through `Av1Block.tx_depth`/`txfm_split_tree`. [Spec: AV1 §6.10.16]

## U

- **Unified `mb_type`** — OVT's single numbering of H.264 macroblock types across I/P/B slices (`AvcMbType`), with `AvcMb.mb_type_raw` keeping the signalled value. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3.1]
- **Unit** — OVT's generic bitstream packet: one NAL unit, one OBU or one raw sample; `table Unit` with `index`, `file_span`, `prefix_span`, `type`, `type_name`, `layer_id`, `temporal_id`, `epb_positions`, `frame`, `header`, `sample_index`. [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3]

- **Unit source policy** — the per-stream decision whether analysis units come from OVT's own container reader (Role A) or from libavformat (Role B). [OVT: chapter 8 §8.1]
- **UpscaledWidth** — AV1 derived variable: the frame width after super-resolution upscaling (= `FrameWidth` when superres is off). [Spec: AV1 §7.21]
- **Upstream-Status** — the OpenEmbedded patch-header convention recording whether a patch was submitted, accepted or is inappropriate for upstream; required on every `third_party/patches/*` file. [OVT: chapter 15 §15.5.5]

- **UVQ** (Universal Video Quality) — Google's no-reference MOS predictor for user-generated content (ContentNet, CompressionNet, DistortionNet, AggregationNet; Apache-2.0), run in OVT through ONNX Runtime from the TensorFlow SavedModels exported via `tf2onnx` (D11); id `uvq`, range 1–5. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.6]

## V

- **vcpkg manifest / baseline** — `vcpkg.json` declaring dependencies and the `builtin-baseline` commit pinning all port versions. [OVT: chapter 13 §13.3.2]

- **Verify mode** — comparing two plugins' outputs (`FrameAnalysis` and planes) and the decoded picture hash SEI; `ovt verify`. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.6.4; chapter 12 §12.2.12]

- **Virtual file** (DASH/HLS) — the logical concatenation of an init segment and media segments addressed by one byte-offset space, presented as a `RandomAccessFile`/`ovt_File` with per-segment provenance (`DashHlsContext.segments[].virtual_span`). [OVT: chapter 8 §8.6.7; chapter 6 §6.4]
- **VMAF** — Netflix's Video Multimethod Assessment Fusion (0–100): an SVM fusing VIF, ADM and motion features. - **VP9 / VVC (H.266) / MPEG-2** — codecs OVT recognises in `Codec` (`VP9`, `H266`, `MPEG2`) for probing via libavformat but does not analyse at block level; VP9 and MPEG-2 also play back via libavcodec, while the core FFmpeg flavour has no VVC decoder (pool-covered, chapter 13 §13.3.3). [OVT: chapter 6 §6.3]

## W

- **Warm decoder** — an analysis decoder instance kept alive between requests so a later frame in the same GOP continues without re-decoding from the RAP. [OVT: chapter 7 §7.5.3]
- **Warped motion** — AV1 local warp (`motion_mode = WARPED_CAUSAL`): a 6-parameter affine model estimated from neighbouring MVs; `Av1MotionMode`, `MotionVector.flags` bit 2. [Spec: AV1 §7.11.3.8]
- **Waveform** — a scope plotting luminance (nits or code value) against horizontal picture position. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.7.6]
- **Wayland colour-management protocol** (`color-management-v1`) — the wayland-protocols (≥ 1.41) extension by which clients describe surface colour (primaries, transfer, HDR metadata) and query output capabilities; OVT's Linux HDR path. [OVT: chapter 10 §10.4.2]
- **Weak copyleft** — LGPL/MPL-style licences that keep the library open but allow linking from differently licensed code; OVT's `core/` is weak-copyleft. [OVT: chapter 15 §15.1]
- **Weighted prediction** — scaling and offsetting reference samples per reference (`pred_weight_table`; explicit or H.264 implicit); `MotionVector.flags` bit 1. [Spec: H.264 §8.4.2.3; H.265 §8.5.3.3.4.3]
- **Width class** — chapter 4's grouping of Android layouts (< 840, 840–1199, ≥ 1200 dp); see *breakpoint* for the normative thresholds. [OVT: chapter 4; chapter 11 §11.5.2]
- **Wiener filter / self-guided filter** — the two AV1 loop-restoration filters: a separable 7-tap symmetric Wiener filter, or a self-guided projection (`sgrproj`) blending two guided-filter outputs with signalled weights. [Spec: AV1 §7.17.4, §7.17.3]
- **Working domain** — the sample representation a metric declares it needs: `WorkingDomain { CodeValue, LinearLight, PU21, ICtCp, ModelRGB }` (CLI/JSON ids `code_value`, `linear_light`, `pu21`, `ictcp`, `model_rgb`); the normaliser converts into it. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.2.5; chapter 6 §6.5]
- **Work item** (WI) — a sized (S/M/L ≤ 1 week), templated task `WI-<phase>.<nnn>` with goal, dependencies, produces, interface, steps and acceptance tests; the unit of planning in chapter 16. [OVT: chapter 0 §0.1 item 6]
- **WorkingDomain** — the sample domain a metric plugin declares it operates in (`code_value`, `linear_light`, `pu21`, `ictcp`, `model_rgb`); the normalisation stage converts frames into it before scoring (`--domain` forces it). [OVT: chapter 9 §9.4; chapter 12 §12.2.9]
- **WPP** (wavefront parallel processing) — HEVC `entropy_coding_sync_enabled_flag`: CABAC state is synchronised from the CTU above-right at each row start so rows decode in parallel; rows become *substreams*. [Spec: H.265 §7.4.3.3, §9.3.1]
- **wPSNR** — the JVET HDR CTC luma-level-weighted PSNR with per-sample weights `2^(clamp(0.015·Y10 − 7.5, −3, 6)/3)`. [OVT: chapter 9 §9.4.10]
- **W^X** (write xor execute) — Android's policy forbidding execution of writable files; shapes OVT's plugin loading rules (plugins must ship inside the APK/feature module). [OVT: chapter 13 §13.4.6]
